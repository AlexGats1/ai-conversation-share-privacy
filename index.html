用途与数据流

本扩展由独立开发者维护，用于选择 AI 网页对话、整理可编辑的摘要，并在本地导出 Markdown、TXT 或 PDF。扩展没有开发者运营的服务器、统计分析服务或广告追踪器；不会把聊天上传给开发者，不出售用户数据，也不用于广告画像。

在您主动读取或选取时，扩展访问当前支持的 AI 网页中的消息文字、角色、图片及必要的页面结构，并尝试加载历史。原网页地址、标签页标识和内容校验值仅用于核对来源、防止把结果写入错误的任务；会在本地暂存这些必要的对话页面地址，但不读取或建立浏览器的完整历史记录列表。您可以选择、移除和遮盖分享副本中的内容，原聊天不被修改。

所选聊天和图片可能包含您或他人的姓名、联系方式、健康、财务或其他敏感信息。扩展将其作为用户选择的网站内容处理，不按这些类别提取身份、建立画像或分析个人状况。请在生成摘要和分享前检查、移除或遮盖不希望提供的内容；扩展不保证自动识别全部敏感信息。

自动摘要会使用原平台

您点击自动生成并授予对应网站权限后，扩展会在同一 AI 平台的新对话中发送选定的文字素材和整理指令；长内容可能分段发送。它使用该网站现有的登录状态或访客额度。图片像素不随摘要指令发送。扩展会读取该对话的回复并放回本地任务。该过程需要网络，受原平台的条款、隐私政策、账户和额度限制影响，不是离线模型推理。关闭或清除扩展任务不会删除平台上已经发送的对话。您也可以使用手动摘要或仅导出原文。

接收方取决于素材来源：Gemini（Google，gemini.google.com）、ChatGPT（OpenAI，chatgpt.com）、Claude（Anthropic，claude.ai）、DeepSeek（chat.deepseek.com），以及千问／Qwen（阿里巴巴，www.qianwen.com／chat.qwen.ai）。仅向本次选定素材的原平台发送，不自动转发给其他 AI 平台。图片请求可能访问相应平台及 Google／OpenAI 图片服务域名（*.googleusercontent.com、encrypted-tbn0/1/2/3.gstatic.com、*.oaiusercontent.com）。这些服务会收到图片请求及正常的网络连接信息，例如 IP 地址；扩展不从中提取位置或用于追踪。

图片与可选网页权限

您确认选段时，扩展会尝试从原网页取得所选回复的图片；若相应网站权限已获准，也会尝试下载图片副本。您还可以点击“补全回复图片”重试并按需授权。下载仅限允许的网页和图片域名。浏览器可能在请求中使用已有登录凭据；扩展不读取密码、Cookie 值或 API key。图片在本地保留为静态副本。备用的“保存可见图片”会临时在内存中截图当前标签页的可见区域，再在本地裁剪到图片区域；仅裁剪后的图片副本保留在任务中。截图瞬间可能包含同一可见区域的其他内容，请在使用前核对页面。

本地保存与删除

选定素材、摘要、图片副本、来源校验信息、生成状态和导出预览存储在浏览器扩展的会话存储中。关闭侧栏可以恢复；关闭所属窗口、清除任务、重启浏览器、重载或卸载扩展会清理相应临时数据，具体取决于操作和浏览器行为。语言、发送长度和导出格式等偏好保存在本地持久存储中，卸载扩展可清除。扩展不使用浏览器同步存储。

您保存的文件由您管理，清除扩展不会删除它们。扩展提供本地诊断记录，包含时间、计数、状态和固定错误码，不包含对话正文、账号信息或完整页面地址。记录不会自动发送。若您主动邮件求助，邮件服务和收件人会收到您的发件地址及自愿提供的内容；请先移除敏感信息。支持邮件只用于处理请求，不用于营销，您可以通过上述邮箱请求删除开发者持有的支持邮件副本。

使用限制与政策变更

本扩展对用户数据的使用遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策，包括 Limited Use（有限使用）要求。

扩展访问和使用的数据仅服务于上述选择、摘要及导出功能。不会将数据转移或出售给第三方用于无关用途，不用于信用评估或放贷，不允许人工查看聊天内容（除非您主动在支持请求中提供）。如政策或数据处理方式改变，将更新本页与版本日期。平台自己的数据处理以及您自行分享的文件由相应平台、收件人和您决定。

AI Conversation Share · Privacy Policy

Version 0.8.1 · Updated October 13, 2026

Support and privacy contact: ngomgabongp@outlook.com

Purpose and data flow

This independently maintained extension selects messages from AI conversation webpages, prepares editable recaps, and exports Markdown, TXT or PDF locally. It has no developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising trackers. It does not upload chats to the developer, sell user data, or use data for advertising profiles.

When you initiate capture or selection, the extension accesses message text, roles, images and necessary page structure on the supported AI webpage, and attempts to load earlier messages. The source address, tab identifiers and content checks are used only to verify the source and prevent updates to the wrong task. These necessary conversation page addresses are temporarily held locally; the extension does not read or build a full browser history list. You can remove messages and redact the sharing copy without changing the original conversation.

Selected chats and images may contain names, contact details, health, financial or other sensitive information about you or others. The extension processes this as user-selected website content; it does not extract identities, build profiles or analyze personal circumstances by those categories. Review, remove or redact unwanted information before generating or sharing. The extension does not automatically identify all sensitive information.

Automatic recaps use the original AI platform

When you start automatic generation and grant access to the relevant website, selected text and recap instructions are sent into a new conversation on the same AI platform. Long material may be sent in multiple parts. The platform's existing login session or guest allowance is used. Image pixels are not sent with recap instructions. The extension retrieves the response into your local task. This requires a network connection and is subject to the platform's terms, privacy policy, account and usage limits; it is not offline model inference. Clearing an extension task does not delete conversations already sent to that platform. Manual recaps and transcript-only export are also available.

The recipient depends on the source: Gemini (Google, gemini.google.com), ChatGPT (OpenAI, chatgpt.com), Claude (Anthropic, claude.ai), DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com), or Qianwen/Qwen (Alibaba, www.qianwen.com / chat.qwen.ai). Only the original platform receives this task; content is not automatically forwarded to other AI platforms. Image requests may contact these platforms and Google/OpenAI image hosts (*.googleusercontent.com, encrypted-tbn0/1/2/3.gstatic.com, *.oaiusercontent.com). Those services receive the image request and ordinary connection information such as your IP address; the extension does not derive location or use this for tracking.

Images and optional website access

When you confirm selected messages, the extension attempts to copy their reply images from the source page. If relevant website access was already granted, it also attempts to download image copies. You can retry using the image-completion button and grant access when prompted. Downloads are limited to permitted website and image domains. The browser may attach existing authentication credentials to requests; the extension does not read passwords, cookie values or API keys. Images are kept as local static copies. The visible-image fallback temporarily captures the visible tab viewport in memory, then crops it locally to the image area. Only the cropped copy is retained in the task. Other visible page content may be present in the temporary screenshot; check the page before using this option.

Local storage and deletion

Selected material, recaps, image copies, source verification information, generation state and export previews use extension session storage. Closing the sidebar preserves the task. Closing its browser window, clearing the task, restarting the browser, reloading or uninstalling the extension removes the corresponding temporary data, depending on the operation and browser behavior. Language, message length and export preferences use persistent local storage and can be removed by uninstalling the extension. Browser sync storage is not used.

Saved files are under your control and are not deleted when extension data is cleared. Optional local diagnostics contain timestamps, counts, states and fixed error codes, without conversation text, account information or full page addresses. They are never sent automatically. If you email support, your mail provider and the recipient receive your sender address and any information you voluntarily include. Remove sensitive details first. Support emails are used to handle requests, not for marketing. Contact the address above to request deletion of support email copies held by the developer.

Limited use and changes

The extension's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data access and use are limited to the selection, recap and export features described above. Data is not sold or transferred for unrelated purposes, used for creditworthiness or lending, or read by a person unless you voluntarily provide it in a support request. Changes to this policy or data handling will be reflected here with an updated date. The original AI platform controls its own data processing, and you control the files you choose to share with others.
